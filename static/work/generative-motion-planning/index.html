PlanCast

A reactive flow-matching motion planner that predicts where obstacles will be.

Paper and code after the review

A small world model inside the planner forecasts, from four recent point-cloud frames, where every scene patch will be over the next 16 steps. The forecast shapes the trajectory, checks every draft and picks the plan.

89 %success with a cube crossing the route at twice hand speed, against 60 % for the same network shown no motion and 56 % for VAMP replanning on the same point clouds
96 %at hand speed, against 62 % for VAMP
58 msmedian per replan on a laptop GPU (RTX 3050)
600held-out MπNets problems, 24 situations, 3 rollout seeds

Everything is simulated: point clouds without occlusion or noise, movers that ignore the arm, and a world that waits while a planner plans. Hand speed: the MπNets expert path's mean hand step on that problem (median 1.2 cm).

How it works

  1. ① the motion feature is gated into the scene tokens
  2. ② between the eight flow passes, a geometric self-check measures each draft against the forecast
  3. ③ the selector keeps the candidate with the fewest predicted contacts, executed for two steps before the next replan
